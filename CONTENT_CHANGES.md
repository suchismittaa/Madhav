## Corrections
1. **Hare Krishna Mahamantra**: the note said the text is "identical in all major traditions". That is wrong about order: the Gaudiya tradition chants Hare Krishna first (as shown), while some recensions/translations of the Kali-Santarana Upanishad print Hare Rama first. Note and explanation corrected; source link added.
2. **Om Namah Shivaya**: the explanation said the mantra "is found in the Shri Rudram". Only the words "namaḥ śivāya" occur there. Reworded.
3. **Rigveda reference** for the Maha Mrityunjaya now also lists the Yajurveda recurrences.
4. Shiva texts moved from *Other Devotions* to the new **Shiva** category.
